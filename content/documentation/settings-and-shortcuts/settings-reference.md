Open settings with **Cmd/Ctrl + ,**, the avatar menu, or the **Open App Settings** quick action. Eleven sections:

## Account/Profile Settings
Your profile and, for admins, your church's details.

- **Profile:** a profile photo you can upload, your **Full name** and **Phone number**.
- **Email:** shows whether your address is verified. To change it, enter the **New email** and your **Current password**, click **Send code**, then type the **Code** sent to the new address and click **Confirm email**. **Resend code** is available after a short wait.
- **Church:** the **Church name**, **Pastor**, **Address** and **Church email**. Admins can edit these and upload a church logo, or click **Use default logo** to remove it. Other members see the details but can't change them.

Your plan, subscription status, **Cancel Subscription** and **Billing History** are in Subscription Settings.

## Subscription Settings
Your plan, billing and payment method. If a payment has failed, a banner offers **Retry payment** and **Update payment method**. See [Plans](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams#if-a-payment-fails). The default **Look and Feel** for new slides: **Default font**, **Default font size** (50–150%), **Default slide alignment (padding up to 200 on every side)**, **Uppercase text**, **Outlined text**, **Bold text**.

## Display Settings
**Control Center** screen info · **Live Window Settings** (Open in fullscreen mode; Close live window when this tab or window is closed; **Let a team member's phone control this output**) · **Secondary Screens** (pick which monitor is the Live display; Refresh screens) · **NDI Live Output** (desktop app only, with an **NDI quality** choice, see [NDI output](/docs/going-live/ndi-output)) · **Stream to YouTube (beta)** (desktop app only, rolling out gradually, see [Streaming to YouTube](/docs/going-live/streaming-to-youtube)). See [Multi-screen setup](/docs/going-live/multi-screen-setup), [using CoW on your phone](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/using-cloud-of-worship-on-your-phone) and [NDI output](/docs/going-live/ndi-output).

**Let a team member's phone control this output** is on by default and is stored on that computer only. It lets someone on a phone pick this device and take slides live on the screen it is driving, but only while the live window is open here, and you can see who is controlling it above the slide schedule.

## Microphone Settings
**Active Microphone**, **Available Microphones**, **Use as default**. Shows **Microphone access denied** if the browser blocks it.

## Slide Settings

![Slide settings for fonts and formatting](https://media.cloudofworship.com/open/marketing/docs/settings-general.webp)
Default font · Default font size · Default lines per slide · **Default line spacing** (Single, Normal or Double) · **Fit each line on one row** (shrinks song and hymn text so each lyric line stays on one row) · Default slide alignment · Default background fill type · Uppercase text · Outlined text · Bold text · Text line background · **Song/hymn title and artistes** (show or hide labels) · **Footnotes and credits** · **Transitions between slides** · **Transition style** (Fade, Drift, Breathe or Cascade, a Teams feature, applies to every slide) · **Transition interval** · **Micro animations**

## Slide Background Settings
Default background per slide type: colour, gradient, or image.

## Blank Settings
**Blank Screen**: completely blank by default, or **Use a video / image background** with a **Video** or **Image**.

## Bible Version Settings
**Default Bible version** and **Available Versions**. Use the search box above the list to find a version by name, country or language. Each version shows its country's flag. **Save** a version to make it available offline.

## Storage Settings
**Cloud storage used** against your plan's limit, a breakdown of stored media groups by size, removal options, and **Upload videos to cloud** (on by default; turn it off if you'd rather keep large videos device-local only. Images and audio are unaffected). Destructive actions require typing **"intentionally deleting"** to confirm.

## Performance Settings
For slower computers. These settings are stored on this computer only, so changing them at the church laptop never changes anything on a teammate's computer.

- **Lite mode:** one switch for slower computers. It turns on **Reduce visual effects**, **Still video in preview** and a **Camera quality** of 720p together. Switch it off to put them back. It leaves camera sharing alone.
- **Reduce visual effects:** drops the blur behind your words and the frosted glass on panels. Slides look a touch flatter and the computer works a lot less.
- **Still video in preview:** the small preview shows one frame of a video while the projector plays it, so your computer decodes the video once, not twice.
- **Keep camera on:** keeps a camera running after its slide leaves the screen, so it is ready the moment you come back to it. Turn it off on slower computers, or to free a capture card for other apps. It applies in every window.
- **Camera quality:** **Auto (up to 1080p)** or **720p**. 720p is a quarter of the work for the camera, the screen and anyone watching it, and still looks sharp on a projector.
- **Share cameras with teammates:** lets teammates see this computer's cameras on their own screens. Each person watching costs this computer a little more work, so switch it off if it is struggling.

## Other Settings
**Notification sounds** (play a chime when a teammate changes the schedule) · **Content Overlay Layout** (Position and Size) · **Overlays & Themes** (Decorative overlay) · **Transcription Settings** (Automatic transcription actions, Voice Bible version changes)

::callout{type="important" title="Defaults apply to new slides only"}
"Look and Feel" defaults apply to **new slides only**. They do not retroactively restyle existing slides. Edit those individually.
::

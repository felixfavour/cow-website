Open settings with **Cmd/Ctrl + ,**, the avatar menu, or the **Open App Settings** quick action. Ten sections:

## Account/Profile Settings
Your profile and, for admins, your church's details.

- **Profile:** a profile photo you can upload, your **Full name** and **Phone number**.
- **Email:** shows whether your address is verified. To change it, enter the **New email** and your **Current password**, click **Send code**, then type the **Code** sent to the new address and click **Confirm email**. **Resend code** is available after a short wait.
- **Church:** the **Church name**, **Pastor**, **Address** and **Church email**. Admins can edit these and upload a church logo, or click **Use default logo** to remove it. Other members see the details but can't change them.

Your plan, subscription status, **Cancel Subscription** and **Billing History** are in Subscription Settings.

## Subscription Settings
Your plan, billing and payment method. If a payment has failed, a banner offers **Retry payment** and **Update payment method**. See [Plans](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams#if-a-payment-fails). The default **Look and Feel** for new slides: **Default font**, **Default font size** (50–150%), **Default slide alignment**, **Uppercase text**, **Outlined text**, **Bold text**.

## Display Settings
**Control Center** screen info · **Live Window Settings** (Open in fullscreen mode; Close live window when this tab or window is closed; **Let a team member's phone control this output**) · **Secondary Screens** (pick which monitor is the Live display; Refresh screens) · **NDI Live Output** (desktop app only). See [Multi-screen setup](/docs/going-live/multi-screen-setup), [using CoW on your phone](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/using-cloud-of-worship-on-your-phone) and [NDI output](/docs/going-live/ndi-output).

**Let a team member's phone control this output** is on by default and is stored on that computer only. It lets someone on a phone pick this device and take slides live on the screen it is driving, but only while the live window is open here, and you can see who is controlling it above the slide schedule.

## Microphone Settings
**Active Microphone**, **Available Microphones**, **Use as default**. Shows **Microphone access denied** if the browser blocks it.

## Slide Settings
Default font · Default font size · Default lines per slide · Default slide alignment · Default background fill type · Uppercase text · Outlined text · Bold text · Text line background · **Song/hymn title and artistes** (show or hide labels) · **Footnotes and credits** · **Transitions between slides** · **Transition interval** · **Micro animations**

## Slide Background Settings
Default background per slide type: colour, gradient, or image.

## Blank Settings
**Blank Screen**: completely blank by default, or **Use a video / image background** with a **Video** or **Image**.

## Bible Version Settings
**Default Bible version** and **Available Versions**. Use the search box above the list to find a version by name. **Save** a version to make it available offline.

## Storage Settings
**Cloud storage used** against your plan's limit, a breakdown of stored media groups by size, removal options, and **Upload videos to cloud** (on by default; turn it off if you'd rather keep large videos device-local only. Images and audio are unaffected). Destructive actions require typing **"intentionally deleting"** to confirm.

## Other Settings
**Alert Settings** (Default alert limit) · **Content Overlay Layout** (Position and Size) · **Overlays & Themes** (Decorative overlay) · **Transcription Settings** (Automatic transcription actions, Voice Bible version changes)

::callout{type="important" title="Defaults apply to new slides only"}
"Look and Feel" defaults apply to **new slides only**. They do not retroactively restyle existing slides. Edit those individually.
::

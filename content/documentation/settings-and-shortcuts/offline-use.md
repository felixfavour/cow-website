Church Wi-Fi drops at the worst moments. Cloud of Worship (CoW) is built to keep the service running when it does: your schedule stays on screen, you can keep promoting slides, and anything you change is saved on your device until the connection returns.

## What you'll see when the connection drops

A **"You are offline"** notice appears: *"CoW will save your work until an internet connection returns."* Click **Ok, got it** to dismiss it and carry on. Nothing is lost.

## What keeps working offline

- **Running the service.** The slides already in your schedule, the [Live Display](/docs/going-live/opening-the-live-display) and the arrow keys keep working.
- **Bible verses** for every translation you've saved, and **hymn lookup**. Bibles and hymns are stored in your browser.
- **Songs you've already saved** to your church, and slides in [My Library](/docs/building-your-service/my-library).
- **Creating and editing slides.** New slides and edits are saved on this device and uploaded automatically when you reconnect.
- **Backgrounds and media** that have already been downloaded to this device.

## What needs a connection

- Searching the shared online song lyrics catalogue.
- Backgrounds and media that haven't been downloaded to this device yet. You'll see *"Background is not available offline yet"*.
- YouTube and Vimeo embeds.
- [Sermon transcription](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/sermon-transcription).
- Seeing teammates' changes, and sending yours to them. See [Working as a team](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/working-as-a-team).

## Prepare before the service

1. **Save your Bible translations.** In **Settings → Bible Version Settings**, click **Save** next to each translation you want offline. Saved versions show as **Saved**, with a progress bar while downloading. **KJV, NIV and NKJV** are bundled.
2. **Open the schedule on the media computer while you're online**, and click through any slides with videos or large images so they're downloaded.
3. **Add media ahead of time.** Media uploads in the background, so a large video added just before the service may not be ready. See [Adding media](/docs/building-your-service/adding-media).
4. For the most dependable setup on Windows, use the [desktop app](/docs/settings-and-shortcuts/desktop-app) or [install CoW as an app](/docs/settings-and-shortcuts/installing-cloud-of-worship).

## After you reconnect

Your changes upload automatically. If something still looks out of date, search the [Quick Actions Pane](/docs/control-center/quick-actions-pane) for:

- **Upload Offline Slides**: pushes slides you made offline to the cloud.
- **Refresh Slides**: pulls in the latest from your team.

Still stuck? See [Sync & team problems](/docs/troubleshooting/sync-and-team-problems).

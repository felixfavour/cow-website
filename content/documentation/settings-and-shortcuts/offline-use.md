CoW keeps working without a connection.

- A **"You are offline"** banner appears: *"CoW will save your work until an internet connection returns."*
- Changes queue locally and push automatically when you reconnect.
- **Bibles and hymns are stored in your browser**, so scripture and hymn lookup keep working offline.

## Prepare in advance

In **Settings → Bible Version Settings**, click **Save** next to each translation you want available offline. Saved versions show as **Saved**, with a progress bar while downloading. Bundled translations include **KJV, NIV and NKJV**.

## After reconnecting

If something looks out of sync after reconnecting, run **Upload Offline Slides** or **Refresh Slides** from the Quick Actions Pane.

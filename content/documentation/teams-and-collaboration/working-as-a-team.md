CoW is real-time collaborative. Everyone signed into the same church works on the same schedules, and changes appear on each other's screens as they are made.

## Inviting people

Click **Invite** in the top bar. You can:

- Enter **email addresses** (comma-separated) and click **Send Invite**, or
- Click **Copy invite link** to share a join link. It changes to **"Copied!"** to confirm.

Existing members are listed with their role, **Admin** or **Member**. The person who created the church is the Admin.

Invitees land on the [dedicated join page](/docs/getting-started/joining-an-existing-church).

::callout{type="important"}
**Invite to Workspace** is a Teams feature. See [Plans: Free vs Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).
::

## What syncs live

- Slide create, update and delete, including batch changes
- Online presence: who else is in the workspace
- Alerts and overlays

## What does not sync: the live output

Taking a slide live is **local to the device that owns the live window**. It does not move anyone else's live display, and a teammate at home clicking a slide will not change what your congregation is looking at.

That is deliberate. An earlier version synced the live slide across every client, which meant any window open on the schedule could move the congregation screen, with no way to say which screen a change was meant for.

There are two ways a slide reaches a screen other than your own:

- **[Livestream viewers](/docs/going-live/livestream-output-obs-vmix)** follow the device that owns the live window, one way.
- **A phone can drive one named output**, chosen explicitly, and only while that computer has its live window open and has left the setting on. See [using CoW on your phone](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/using-cloud-of-worship-on-your-phone).

## Phones are full team members

On a Teams plan, someone on a phone is a full operator, not a viewer. They can build slides, switch schedules and, if you let them, take slides live on the projector at the media desk. See [using CoW on your phone](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/using-cloud-of-worship-on-your-phone).

## Slide locking

When someone is editing a slide, CoW locks it so two operators can't overwrite each other. If a slide is locked you'll be told rather than silently losing your change.

::callout{type="tip" title="Changes not showing up?"}
Check for the offline banner. If you're online, run **Refresh Slides** to re-fetch from the server. More in [Sync & team problems](/docs/troubleshooting/sync-and-team-problems).
::

*Teams feature, with 10 free minutes on Free.* Open with **Transcribe Sermon**.

> *"Transcribe Sermon turns your microphone into a live note-taker; capturing what's being said and surfacing relevant Bible passages automatically as you preach."*

## Three things it does

| Capability | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Live transcription** | Words appear in real time as you speak, with Bible references highlighted |
| **Auto-detect scriptures** | Any verse mentioned (for example "John 3:16") is instantly added to the **Scriptures** tab. Click it to open the slide |
| **AI scripture suggestions** | Related verses surface even when you don't quote them directly |

The panel has two tabs, **Transcripts** and **Scriptures**. Before anything is detected the Scriptures tab reads *"Scriptures matching the sermon will appear here automatically."*

## How much you can transcribe

Teams includes **3 hours of transcription every week**, and the limit resets every Monday. Minutes you don't use roll over into the next week, so a quiet week leaves you up to 6 hours for the one after. Rollover only carries one week: minutes that were rolled over don't roll again. A church on Free gets **10 minutes** once, to try it on a real sermon. The panel shows how many free minutes are left, and the timer turns amber when you're close to the end. Once the free minutes are used, transcription is part of Teams.

## Setup

Choose your input in **Settings → Microphone Settings**, which lists **Available Microphones** and lets you mark one **Use as default**. If the browser blocks access you'll see **"Microphone access denied"**. Grant mic permission in your browser's site settings.

## Related toggles

In **Settings → Other Settings**:

- **Automatic transcription actions**
- **Voice Bible version changes**: switch translation by voice

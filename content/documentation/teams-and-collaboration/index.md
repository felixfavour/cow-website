Cloud of Worship is built for church media teams. Invite your volunteers to the same church, prepare slides together in real time from different computers, and control the projector from a phone at the media desk. This section also covers live sermon transcription and what the Free and Teams plans include.

To bring your team in, start with [Working as a team](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/working-as-a-team).

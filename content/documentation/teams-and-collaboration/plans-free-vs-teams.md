## Trying Teams features on Free

Many Teams features open on the Free plan so you can see what you'd get before paying. Slide Templates, alerts, countdowns, interludes and YouTube or Vimeo embeds all open their panel as normal, with a small Teams notice at the top. Fill the panel in as you like. The upgrade prompt only appears when you click the final **Create**, **Add** or **Send** button.

One-click actions with nothing to preview, such as time slides, removing an alert and the stage clock controls, show the upgrade prompt straight away.

## Free allowances

Three features are metered on Free rather than locked, so you can use them for real:

| Feature | Free allowance | How it's counted |
|---------|----------------|------------------|
| **Search song lyrics** | 10 songs a month | Search is free. Adding a song from the results to your schedule uses one. The count resets at midnight UTC on the 1st of each month. |
| **Sermon transcription** | 10 minutes | A one-off allowance per church. The panel shows how many free minutes are left. |
| **Livestream URL** | 5 sessions | Streaming a schedule on a new day uses one session. The **Go Live** menu shows how many are left. |

When an allowance runs out, the feature shows the upgrade prompt. Allowances belong to the church, so every member draws from the same count.

::callout{type="important" title="Your plan comes from your church"}
Your plan comes from your **church** record, not your personal user record. When an admin upgrades the church, every member gets Teams features.
::

::callout{type="warning" title="Expiry heads-up"}
If your Teams subscription is within **7 days of expiring**, a banner appears across the top of the Control Center.
::

## Upgrading

Use the **Upgrade Plan** quick action, or go to **App Settings → Subscription Settings**. Current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

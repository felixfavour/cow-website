## The top bar

| Element | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Schedule name** (centre) | Click to rename the current schedule inline |
| **Chevron ⌄** | Opens the schedule switcher and the **Create a schedule** dialog |
| **Invite** | Opens the invite modal for your church workspace |
| **Theme toggle** | Light / dark mode |
| **Avatar** (far right) | Your account menu: church name, **App Settings**, **Shortcut & Hotkeys**, **Sign out** |

## The Live Output Preview

*Top-right pane.* A miniature mirror of exactly what your congregation sees right now, with the slide title and a **LIVE** badge. When nothing is live it shows the blank or idle screen.

Use the expand icon in its bottom-right corner to enlarge it.

The Cloud of Worship interface is divided into three main sections:

1. **Quick Actions Pane** (left)
2. **Preview and Edit Content Pane** (centre)
3. **Slide Schedule Pane** (bottom right)

Each section is designed to simplify controlling your church's on-screen projection and let you multitask effectively.

![The three-pane Control Center](/images/docs/02-operator-dashboard.webp)

## Resizing the panes

The left and right panes are **resizable**. Drag the divider between panes and CoW remembers your widths between sessions (within built-in min and max limits). The Preview and Edit Content pane can also be resized vertically between its grid and editor halves.

## The panes in detail

| Pane | What it's for | Read more |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| **Quick Actions** | Every tool and action, searchable | [Quick Actions Pane](/docs/control-center/quick-actions-pane) |
| **Preview and Edit Content** | Modify, edit and format slides before they go live | [Preview and Edit Pane](/docs/control-center/preview-and-edit-pane) |
| **Slide Schedule** | Your running order. Click a slide to take it live | [Slide Schedule Pane](/docs/control-center/slide-schedule-pane) |
| **Top bar and Live Output Preview** | Schedule switching, invites, theme, account, and a mirror of the live screen | [Top Bar & Live Preview](/docs/control-center/top-bar-and-live-preview) |

::callout{type="important"}
Slides appear in the Slide Schedule as soon as they're added, but they **will not go live until you explicitly promote them** to the Live Display.
::

*Bottom-right pane.*

The Slide Schedule Pane shows all the slides that are ready to be promoted to the live display. Each slide is presented with:

- a **small preview** of the slide,
- a **unique slide name**,
- a **slide type** badge (Text, Media, Song, Hymn, Setlist, Bible…).

## Promoting a slide

**To promote a slide to the live display, simply click on it.** The projected slide is marked with a red **LIVE** badge.

![A slide live with the LIVE badge](/images/docs/13-go-live-active.webp)

## Row actions

Hovering a row reveals two icons on the right:

| Icon | Action |
|------|--------|
| ✏️ **Pencil** | *Preview / Edit Slide*: opens it in the Preview and Edit Content Pane |
| 🗑️ **Trash** | *Delete slide*: confirms first |

## Live controls

The pane header holds the two live controls: **Blank** and **Go Live**. See [Opening the Live Display](/docs/going-live/opening-the-live-display) and [Blank screen](/docs/going-live/blank-screen).

::callout{type="tip"}
**Drag a song slide onto a Song Setlist slide** in this pane to add it to that setlist. See [Song Setlists](/docs/building-your-service/song-setlists).
::

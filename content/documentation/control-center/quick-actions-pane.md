*Left pane.*

The Quick Actions Pane is where you'll find all the tools and actions you can perform in Cloud of Worship, presented in an easy-to-search format. Sometimes you want to accomplish a task but can't remember the exact steps. The Quick Actions Pane makes everything searchable, so you don't have to memorise the process.

## Default and searchable actions

- **Default actions:** the most commonly used actions are shown by default: Display Bible, Search Whole Bible, Display Hymns, Transcribe Sermon, Add Song, My Library, Search song lyrics, Song Setlist, Create Text Slide, Add Media and Slide Templates.
- **Searchable actions:** *everything* is indexed, including actions not in the default list: "Go to app settings", "Take screen live", "Force sync" / **Refresh Slides**, "Create new schedule", "Sign out", "Upgrade Plan", "What's New", **Join Community**, and every individual settings page.

The full list is in the [Quick Actions reference](/docs/control-center/quick-actions-reference).

## The search box searches everything

Type into the search box and CoW searches your **actions, the Bible, your songs, your library and the hymnal simultaneously**.

![Quick Actions search returning actions, scripture, songs and hymns together](/images/docs/05-display-hymns.webp)

Results are grouped under filter chips (**Bible · Song lyrics · Hymns · Library · Actions · More**) so you can narrow a busy result set.

::callout{type="tip" title="Jump to search from anywhere"}
Press **`/`** anywhere in the app to jump straight into the Quick Actions search. **Cmd/Ctrl + K** works too.
::

::callout{type="tip"}
The placeholder rotates through real examples (*"Matt 28:19"*, *"Amazing Grace"*, *"Way Maker"*, *"kids pickup alert"*, *"30s timer"*, *"motion background"*), a good hint at the range of things you can type.
::

## Navigating inside an action

Choosing an action switches the pane into that action's own view with a breadcrumb at the top, for example **Quick Actions › Bible**. Click **Quick Actions** in the breadcrumb, or the **✕**, to go back.

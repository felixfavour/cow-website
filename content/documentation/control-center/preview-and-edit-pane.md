*Centre pane.*

The Preview and Edit Content Pane is what we like to call the **"processing and refinement centre"**. This is where you modify, edit and format slides before promoting them to the live display.

## Two halves

| Half | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Top: the slide grid** | Every slide in the active schedule as a thumbnail, tagged with its type (Text, Media, Song, Hymn, Setlist…). Click one to select it. |
| **Bottom: the editor** | The selected slide's editor. Until you select something it reads *"Select slide above to start editing"*. |

Slides in this pane are highly flexible. You can make changes **even while they are being displayed live**, applying unique modifications to individual slides in real time without disrupting your presentation. See [Editing a slide](/docs/building-your-service/editing-a-slide).

::callout{type="important"}
Slides appear in the Slide Schedule as soon as they are added to this pane, but they **will not go live until you explicitly promote them** to the Live Display.
::

## The slide card menu (⋮)

Hover a thumbnail and click **⋮**:

| Menu item | What it does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Duplicate Slide** | Copy it within the same schedule |
| **Duplicate as Overlay** | Copy it as an overlay layer that shows *on top of* the live slide instead of replacing it |
| **Save to Library** | Save to My Library for reuse in any future schedule |
| **Save as Template** | Save its styling as a reusable template |
| **Delete Slide** | Remove it. Asks to confirm, and is not reversible |

## Select Slides (bulk mode)

Click **Select Slides** in the pane header to act on many slides at once. You can select all, or clear the selection and exit bulk mode.

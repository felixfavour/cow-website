The Control Center is the screen you work in during the week and on Sunday. It has three panes: **Quick Actions** on the left for creating slides and searching everything, **Preview and Edit Content** in the middle for editing the selected slide, and the **Slide Schedule** for your running order. Above them sit the top bar and a live preview of what's on the projector.

These guides walk through each pane, and the [Quick Actions reference](/docs/control-center/quick-actions-reference) lists every action and the plan it needs.

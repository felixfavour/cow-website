How to get your slides onto the projector and keep the service running smoothly. Open the Live Display, put slides on screen with a click or the arrow keys, and set up a second screen or a stage display for your musicians and preacher. When you need to, blank the screen, send an alert over the slide, or send the output to your livestream in OBS or vMix, or over NDI.

Setting up for the first time? Start with [Opening the live display](/docs/going-live/opening-the-live-display) and [Multi-screen setup](/docs/going-live/multi-screen-setup).

Sometimes you need to tell the room something without taking the lyrics or the sermon point off the screen: a parent needed at the children's church, a car blocking the gate, a reminder about the next event. Alerts and overlays sit **on top of** the live slide, so the service carries on underneath.

| | Alert | Overlay |
|---|-------|---------|
| **Looks like** | A coloured banner that scrolls across the top or bottom | A text or countdown box placed anywhere on screen, like a lower-third |
| **Made from** | The **Add Banners/Alert** panel | An existing text or countdown slide |
| **Best for** | Short, urgent notices | Speaker names, a countdown to the next segment, a standing reminder |
| **Plan** | Teams | Teams |

Free churches can open the alert panel to try it; sending an alert live needs [Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).

## Alerts (Teams)

![Alert configuration](/images/docs/12-add-banner-alert.webp)

### Send an alert

1. Click **Add Banners/Alert** in the [Quick Actions Pane](/docs/control-center/quick-actions-pane).
2. Pick a **Banner color**.
3. Choose an **Alert position**: **Top** or **Bottom**.
4. Type **Your alert content**.
5. Set the scroll **Speed** with the slider, from **Slow** to **Fast**.
6. Click **Send alert to LIVE**.

The alert scrolls across the [Live Display](/docs/going-live/opening-the-live-display) over whatever slide is showing.

### The alert schedule

Alerts you've sent are kept in an **Alert schedule** at the top of the panel, so you can send the same notice again with one click instead of retyping it. Only one alert is live at a time, marked with the live dot. The counter shows how many you've saved out of your limit, which you can change from 5 to 30 in **Settings → Other Settings → Default alert limit** (5 by default).

### Remove an alert

Search for **Remove Alert** in the Quick Actions Pane to clear the banner in one click.

## Overlays (Teams)

An **overlay** shows a slide *on top of* the current live slide instead of replacing it. Use it for lower-thirds such as a speaker's name, a giving reminder, or a countdown to the next part of the service.

### Make an overlay

Overlays are made from **text slides** (with the full-text or heading-and-subtitle layout) and **countdown slides**.

1. Create the slide first. See [Text slides](/docs/building-your-service/text-slides) or [Countdown timers](/docs/building-your-service/countdown-timers).
2. Open the slide's **⋮** menu and choose **Duplicate as Overlay**.

### Show and clear an overlay

- **Double-click** the overlay slide in your schedule to show it, or select it and click **Show Overlay**.
- Click the overlay in the live preview, or **Clear Overlay**, to take it off.

### Overlay settings

In **Settings → Other Settings**:

- **Position**: top left, top middle, top right, middle, bottom left, bottom middle or bottom right.
- **Size** of the overlay box.
- **Decorative overlay**: an optional seasonal effect, such as **Falling Snow**.

::callout{type="tip" title="Livestreaming?"}
Alerts and overlays are part of the live output, so they also appear in [OBS or vMix](/docs/going-live/livestream-output-obs-vmix). Keep alerts meant only for the room short, or clear them before a stream segment.
::

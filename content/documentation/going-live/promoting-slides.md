Three ways to put a slide on screen:

1. **Click the slide** in the Slide Schedule Pane.
2. Click **Go Live** inside the slide's editor.
3. Keyboard: **Cmd/Ctrl + P** for the slide in the editor, or **Cmd/Ctrl + [number]** by position.

![A slide live with the LIVE badge](/images/docs/13-go-live-active.webp)

The live slide is marked **LIVE** in both the Slide Schedule Pane and the Live Output Preview. Clicking a different slide switches instantly.

::callout{type="tip" title="The fastest way to run a service"}
**↑** and **↓** move to the previous or next slide in the schedule and promote it. Once your order is set, you can run the whole service from the arrow keys.
::

## Schedule shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **↓** | Send the next slide in the schedule live |
| **↑** | Send the previous slide in the schedule live |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + 1…9** | Send that numbered slide live |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + 0** | Send the last slide in the schedule live |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + P** | Promote the slide you're previewing |
| **B** | Blank the live output |

Inside a scripture, song or hymn slide, **←** and **→** move between verses.

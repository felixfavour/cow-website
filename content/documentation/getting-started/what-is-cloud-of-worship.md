Cloud of Worship (CoW) is presentation software for churches. You build a slide schedule for a service (songs, scripture, hymns, sermon notes, media) in one window, and project it full-screen on a second display for your congregation.

## Two windows, one service

The app runs in **two windows at once**:

| Window | URL | Who sees it |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| **Control Center** | `app.cloudofworship.com` | You, the operator |
| **Live Display** | `app.cloudofworship.com/live` | Your congregation (projector or second screen) |

Everything you do happens in the Control Center. Nothing reaches the Live Display until you explicitly promote it.

::callout{type="important" title="Same browser, both windows"}
The Control Center and Live Display talk to each other through the browser itself. Open both in the **same browser**. Running the Control Center in Chrome and the live window in Safari will not work.
::

## No install required

Cloud of Worship runs entirely in your browser.

- **URL:** `app.cloudofworship.com`
- **Browsers:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Chrome or Edge are recommended if you want automatic multi-screen detection.
- **Installable:** you can install CoW to your dock or taskbar as an app. See [Installing Cloud of Worship](/docs/settings-and-shortcuts/installing-cloud-of-worship).

You'll normally run it on a laptop connected to a projector or second monitor.

If a teammate invites you, your invite link opens a **dedicated join page** rather than the normal 5-step wizard. It shows:

- **"Join [Church name], [Church type] on Cloud of Worship"**
- The avatars of people already in the workspace, and a line like *"Favour Felix and others already joined"*

## Creating your account from an invite

You fill in a single short form (**Full name**, **Email address**, **Choose a password**) and click **Create your account**.

Because the church already exists, you skip the church-creation and team-invite steps entirely and land straight in the shared workspace.

::callout{type="tip"}
On this page the password hint is the simpler *"at least 8 characters and include a letter and a number."*
::

## Getting an invite

Ask an existing member to click **Invite** in the Control Center's top bar. They can email you directly or copy an invite link. See [Working as a Team](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/working-as-a-team).

Signing up is a **5-step guided wizard**, not one long form. Your progress is shown as five dashes at the bottom of the screen from Step 2 onward.

## Step 1: Get started (credentials)

![Get Started, sign-up step 1](/images/docs/16-signup-step1.webp)

Choose one:

- **Sign up with Google**: one click. Google accounts arrive **pre-verified**, so you skip email verification entirely.
- **Email and password**: enter your email and choose a password.

**Password rules:** at least **8 characters**, including **a letter, a number and a symbol**. If it doesn't match you'll see *"Password must include a letter, a number and a symbol"*. **Continue** stays disabled until both fields are valid.

::callout{type="important" title="This step creates your account immediately"}
There's no final "submit everything" at the end of the wizard. Double-check your email before continuing. If it's already registered you'll see *"Email linked to an account"*. Use **Sign in** instead.
::

## Step 2: What should we call you?

Enter your **Full name**. Teammates see this in the Invite list, in presence indicators, and on slides you have locked for editing.

Signed up with Google? This is **pre-filled** from your Google display name. Just hit Continue.

## Step 3: Tell us about your church

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Are you creating this account for a church?** | Toggle. Switch it **off** for a non-church organization, and every label on the step changes from "church" to "organization". |
| **Church name** | Searchable dropdown of known churches. |
| **Enter church name** | Appears only if you pick **"Other Church (not included)"** or turn the toggle off. Free text. |
| **Church type** | Dropdown (see below). |
| **Head of ministry** | Your pastor or ministry lead. |

**Church type options:** Headquarters · Provincial Headquarters · Zonal Parish · Area / District Parish · Branch · Campus Parish · Online Campus · House Fellowship / Center

All three of church name, type and head of ministry are required before **Continue** activates.

::callout{type="tip" title="Don't create a duplicate church"}
If a teammate already created your church in CoW, **search for it rather than creating a duplicate**. Joining the existing church shares schedules, songs and library items with your team from day one.
::

If you're already signed in but have no church, CoW routes you straight to this step.

## Step 4: Will anyone be joining now? (optional)

Type a teammate's email and click **Add** (or press **Enter**). Each becomes a removable chip. Add as many as you like, then **Continue** to send all invitations at once.

Not ready? Click **"I'll do this later"**.

## Step 5: How can we reach you? (optional)

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Your phone number** | Phone input with country picker. Required for the primary **Continue** button. |
| **How did you find us?** | Optional: Google Search · Social Media · Word of Mouth / Friend · YouTube · Church Community / WhatsApp Group · App Store · Other |

Both are skippable. **"I'll do this later"** finishes onboarding immediately.

## Step 6: Verify your account

Email and password sign-ups get a verification code by email. Google sign-ups skip this entirely.

1. **Enter the code**: 6 characters, auto-uppercased as you type.
2. **Didn't get it?** Click **Resend code**. There's a **120-second cooldown** (counted down inside the input) and a maximum of **3 resends**.
3. **Wrong email?** Click your email address in the subtitle to make it editable, fix it, and click **Save email**. CoW updates your account and sends a fresh code automatically.

::callout{type="tip"}
A code is sent automatically the moment you land here. Check your inbox and spam before hitting Resend.
::

## Step 7: Choose a plan

After verification, CoW shows the **plan chooser** (Free or Teams). Dismiss it and decide later if you prefer. You can change plans anytime in **App Settings → Subscription Settings**. See [Plans: Free vs Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).

## The full flow at a glance

```
Step 1            Step 2       Step 3          Step 4         Step 5        Step 6      Step 7
Credentials  →    Your name →  Your church  →  Invite team →  Contact   →   Verify  →   Pick a
(Google/email)                                 (skippable)    (skippable)   email       plan
     │                                                                        │
     └── Google sign-up ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
         (pre-verified, skips Step 6)
```

You land in the Control Center with an empty account. The Slide Schedule reads **"No slides yet"** until you create your first schedule. Next: [the guided tour](/docs/getting-started/the-guided-tour), then [Schedules](/docs/building-your-service/schedules).

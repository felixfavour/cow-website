## Signing in

Go to `app.cloudofworship.com/login` and use **Google** or your **email and password**.

## Forgot your password

1. Click **Forgot password** on the login screen.
2. On the **"Forgot password?"** page, enter the email linked to your account. *"A reset link will be sent to this address."*
3. Click **Continue** and check your inbox.

## Resetting it

The emailed link opens the **"Reset password"** page. Enter your **New password** and **Confirm password**, then submit.

- Password must be **at least 8 characters and include a letter and a number**.
- If the link is incomplete or expired you'll see *"This password reset link is missing required information"* with a **Request new link** button.

## Signing out

Use the **Sign out** item in the avatar menu, or the **Sign Out** quick action. You'll briefly see a *"Signing out"* screen while your session ends.

New to Cloud of Worship? Start here. These guides take you from signing up to a Bible verse on your church projector: creating your church account or joining one through an invite, signing in, the guided tour, and the two windows you work with. The Control Center is where you prepare and run the service, and the Live Display is what your congregation sees.

Most churches are presenting within five minutes. If you only read one page, read [Your first five minutes](/docs/getting-started/your-first-five-minutes).

Something not working the way you expect? These pages cover the problems church media teams run into most: a live display that stays black or won't open on the projector, sign-in and verification issues, changes that don't sync between teammates, media that won't upload, and microphone problems with transcription. There's also an honest list of known limitations, so nothing catches you out mid-service.

Can't find your problem here? [Contact support](/contact) and we'll help.

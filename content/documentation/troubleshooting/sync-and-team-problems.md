## "You are offline" banner

CoW is queuing your work locally. Nothing is lost. It syncs on reconnect. Click **Ok, got it** to dismiss. See [Offline use](/docs/settings-and-shortcuts/offline-use).

## A teammate's changes aren't showing up

Check for the offline banner. If you're online, run **Refresh Slides** to re-fetch from the server.

## A slide won't let me edit it

Another operator has it locked. Wait, or edit a different slide. See [Slide locking](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/working-as-a-team#slide-locking).

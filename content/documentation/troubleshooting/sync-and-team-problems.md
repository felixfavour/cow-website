Cloud of Worship (CoW) syncs every change to your church in real time. When something looks out of step between two computers, work through the matching section below.

## "You are offline" banner

CoW has lost its internet connection and is saving your work on this device. Nothing is lost: it uploads automatically when you reconnect. Click **Ok, got it** to dismiss the notice and keep presenting.

If the banner keeps coming back, check the computer's Wi-Fi or network cable. For what does and doesn't work without a connection, see [Offline use](/docs/settings-and-shortcuts/offline-use).

## A teammate's changes aren't showing up

1. **Check for the offline banner** on both computers. Changes can't arrive until both are online.
2. **Check you're on the same schedule.** Changes only appear in the schedule they were made in. The schedule name is in the [top bar](/docs/control-center/top-bar-and-live-preview).
3. **Run Refresh Slides.** Search for it in the [Quick Actions Pane](/docs/control-center/quick-actions-pane) to re-fetch every slide from the server.

## Slides I made offline aren't on other computers

Search the Quick Actions Pane for **Upload Offline Slides**. It pushes slides saved on this device up to the cloud. Then run **Refresh Slides** on the other computer.

## A slide won't let me edit it

When you try to edit, you'll see *"Slide is being edited by …"*. Another operator has the slide open, and CoW locks it so two people can't overwrite each other. Wait for them to finish, ask them to move to a different slide, or edit another slide in the meantime. See [Slide locking](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/working-as-a-team#slide-locking).

## A teammate can't see Teams features

Plans belong to the church, not the person. If one operator sees features greyed out, check they joined **your** church through an invite rather than creating a new one. See [Joining an existing church](/docs/getting-started/joining-an-existing-church) and [Plans: Free vs Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).

## Still stuck?

[Contact support](/contact) with the name of the schedule and roughly when the problem started, and we'll look into it.

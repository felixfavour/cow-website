## "Email linked to an account" during sign-up

That email already has a CoW account. Use **Sign in**, or [reset your password](/docs/getting-started/signing-in-and-password-recovery).

## My verification code isn't arriving

Check spam. A code is sent automatically when you land on the verify page, so you may already have one. **Resend** up to 3 times with a 2-minute wait. If the address was wrong, click it on the verify screen to edit it, and a fresh code goes to the corrected address.

## "This password reset link is missing required information"

The link was incomplete or expired. Click **Request new link** and start over from **Forgot password**.

## Teams features are greyed out after upgrading

Your plan comes from the **church** record. Sign out and back in so CoW re-reads it, or confirm the subscription in **Settings → Account/Profile Settings**. See [Plans: Free vs Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).

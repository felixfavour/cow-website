## "Hymn not found" / "We couldn't find that hymn"

Try the hymn **number**, or a distinctive lyric, rather than the full title.

## Microphone access denied (transcription)

Grant microphone permission for `app.cloudofworship.com` in your browser's site settings, then pick your input in **Settings → Microphone Settings**. See [Sermon transcription](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/sermon-transcription).

## My background image or video won't upload

Check the size against your plan's cap. Free allows 3 MB images and 250 MB videos. See [Slide backgrounds](/docs/building-your-service/slide-backgrounds).

## "Some pages didn't upload"

If the connection drops while you import a PDF or add images, CoW keeps the files on your computer and tells you how many pages didn't reach the cloud. They still show on your screen, but not on other devices or for your team yet. CoW retries in the background, and again when the connection comes back or the schedule is next opened, so you don't need to add them again.

## Transcription stopped: "Your free minutes are used"

Free churches get 10 transcription minutes once. On Teams the limit is 3 hours a week, plus whatever you didn't use the week before, and it resets every Monday. See [Sermon transcription](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/sermon-transcription#how-much-you-can-transcribe).

## "Hymn not found" / "We couldn't find that hymn"

Try the hymn **number**, or a distinctive lyric, rather than the full title.

## Microphone access denied (transcription)

Grant microphone permission for `app.cloudofworship.com` in your browser's site settings, then pick your input in **Settings → Microphone Settings**. See [Sermon transcription](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/sermon-transcription).

## My background image or video won't upload

Check the size against your plan's cap. Free allows 3 MB images and 250 MB videos. See [Slide backgrounds](/docs/building-your-service/slide-backgrounds).

A **Song Setlist** is a single slide holding **several songs in order**, so you can move through a worship medley without juggling separate slides.

![Song Setlist builder](/images/docs/08-song-setlist.webp)

## Creating one

1. Click **Song Setlist**. A new setlist slide is created, reading *"Add songs to this setlist from the song list."*
2. Add songs (below).
3. The card title tracks its contents: **"Song Setlist 1 (0 songs)"** → **"(3 songs)"**.

## Adding songs

- **Drag a song slide** from the Slide Schedule Pane **onto the setlist slide**, or
- Add songs from the song list or My Library while the setlist is selected.

## Reordering songs

The songs in a setlist appear as cards across the top of the Preview & Edit Pane. Drag a card to move that song. The song that is showing stays active wherever you move it, and each song keeps its place in its own verses. Click a card to jump to that song, or use its trash icon to remove it.

## Running a setlist live

The setlist tracks two positions at once: **which song** is active, and **which verse** of that song is showing. **←** and **→** move through verses, rolling into the next song at the end. The slide title updates to the current verse (for example "Verse 2").

## Setlist vs. individual song slides

| | Individual song slides | Song Setlist |
|---|---|---|
| Slides in schedule | One per song | One for the whole set |
| Reordering mid-service | Move slides around | Reorder within the setlist |
| Best for | A song at one point in the service | A back-to-back worship medley |

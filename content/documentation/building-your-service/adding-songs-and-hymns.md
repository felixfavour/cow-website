## Adding a song

![Add a song](/images/docs/06-add-song.webp)

1. Click **Add Song**.
2. Fill in **Title** and **Artist**.
3. Paste the **Lyrics**.
4. Save. The song lands in [My Library](/docs/building-your-service/my-library), reusable in any future schedule.

::callout{type="tip" title="Break lyrics into verses"}
Add an **empty line** if you wish to forcefully break your lyrics into verses. Especially useful for building a worship lineup: each blank line starts a new slide.
::

There's also a **Share this song with other users?** option when saving.

## Searching song lyrics

Use **Search song lyrics** to pull lyrics for a song you haven't saved. Matching words are highlighted in the results, and hovering a result scrolls its preview to the line that best matches your search. When a song comes up because of a lyric line rather than its title, that line is shown under the song in place of the artist, so you can see why it matched.

*Teams feature, with 50 free songs a month on Free.* Searching and previewing is always free. On the Free plan, adding a song from the results to your schedule uses one of the month's **50 library songs**, and the panel shows how many are left. The count resets at midnight UTC on the 1st of each month. Teams has no limit.

## Adding a song fast

Click a song and a spinner shows until it lands in the schedule. Songs your church has used before are added instantly, even offline. Clicking twice no longer adds a song twice.

## Languages and translations

When you add or edit a song in **My Library**, open **Language & translations**:

1. Under **Sung in**, start typing the language the song is sung in.
2. Under **Translated into**, add up to **8** languages and type or paste the translation for each. Write one line for each sung line, in the same order. Blank lines and labels like *Chorus* are skipped.
3. Review the translations line by line before you save. CoW warns if a translated line has no sung line to go under, or a sung line has no translation yet (it will show without one).

If you paste lyrics that already have the English in brackets after each line, CoW offers to move it into an English translation for you during the review. It leaves brackets alone when the song is sung in English.

To show a translation on screen, select the song slide and click **Show translation** in the toolbar. It is on by default when the song has one. The translation sits under each lyric line. Opening a song slide picks up lyric and translation fixes without pressing Refresh. If a translation is wrong, use **Report an issue** in the editor, see [Editing a slide](/docs/building-your-service/editing-a-slide#report-an-issue).

## Hymns

Click **Display Hymns** and search by **title or hymn number**. No match shows *"We couldn't find that hymn"*. Try the number or a distinctive lyric.

## Running several songs together

For a back-to-back worship medley, group songs in a [Song Setlist](/docs/building-your-service/song-setlists) instead of separate slides.

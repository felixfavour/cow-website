![Media picker](/images/docs/10-add-media.webp)

**Add Media** has two tabs:

- **Files**: drag and drop, paste from your file explorer, or click to browse images, video and audio.
- **YouTube/Vimeo**: paste a video URL to embed. YouTube and Vimeo embeds are a **Teams** feature.

::callout{type="warning" title="Add media ahead of the service"}
Media is stored locally first and uploaded in the background. Adding a large video right before it's needed is risky. Add media ahead of the service where you can.
::

## Upload limits

Limits depend on your plan. See [Slide backgrounds](/docs/building-your-service/slide-backgrounds#upload-limits-depend-on-your-plan) and [Plans: Free vs Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).

## Media shortcuts

While a media slide is selected, **M** mutes or unmutes it and **Space** plays or pauses it.

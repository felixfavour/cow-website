**Add Media** has two tabs:

- **Files**: drag and drop, paste from your file explorer, or click to browse images, video and audio.
- **YouTube/Vimeo**: paste a video URL to embed. YouTube and Vimeo embeds are a **Teams** feature.

::callout{type="warning" title="Add media ahead of the service"}
Media is stored locally first and uploaded in the background. Adding a large video right before it's needed is risky. Add media ahead of the service where you can.
::

## Uploading with teammates

A media or presentation slide reaches your teammates as soon as you add it, with a progress bar: **Uploading** on the computer sending it, **Syncing** on everyone else's. While it uploads, teammates can't select, edit, pair or take it live. If an upload goes quiet for 2 minutes, or its owner leaves, the card shows **Stayed behind**. Its owner can click **Retry upload** on the card. Deleting a slide mid-upload cancels the upload.

## Volume

Select a video, audio file or YouTube or Vimeo slide and click **Volume** in the editor toolbar. It has a mute switch and a level slider, and the Live Display uses that level. **Seek** opens a track so you can jump to any point in the video or audio.

## Upload limits

Limits depend on your plan. See [Slide backgrounds](/docs/building-your-service/slide-backgrounds#upload-limits-depend-on-your-plan) and [Plans: Free vs Teams](/docs/teams-and-collaboration/plans-free-vs-teams).

## Media shortcuts

While a media slide is selected, **M** mutes or unmutes it and **Space** plays or pauses it.

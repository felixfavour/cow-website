*Teams feature.* A slide pair puts **two slides on the Live Output at once**. Every pair includes a [camera slide](/docs/building-your-service/camera-slides), so you can show the speaker next to the scripture, or the choir next to the lyrics.

::callout{type="note" title="Rolling out gradually"}
Slide pairs depend on camera slides, which are being switched on in stages. If you don't see **Add Camera**, you won't see pairing either.
::

## Make a pair

1. Add a camera slide to the schedule.
2. Open the camera slide's **⋯** menu and choose **Start a pair**.
3. Pick the other slide from the list. Use **Search slides** to find it.

Alternatively, drag a slide onto a camera slide in the schedule. Only camera slides accept a drop. A pair can be a camera and one other slide, or two cameras. A slide can only be in one pair at a time.

To swap the partner later, use **Change paired slide** in the camera's **⋯** menu. To split them, choose **Unpair Slides**.

## Choose a layout

Select either slide in the pair and open **Pair settings** in the Preview & Edit Pane. The camera always takes the picture's place in the layout, whatever the other slide is. With two cameras, the first in the pair does.

| Layout | What the congregation sees |
|--------|----------------------------|
| **Side by side** | The camera in a card beside the other slide, on the pair background |
| **Picture in picture** | The other slide full screen, the camera in a small card |
| **Caption** | The camera full screen, the other slide across the bottom |
| **Lower third** | The camera full screen, the other slide in a box across the bottom |

## Running a pair

- **Advancing:** Up and Down, media controls and the Stage Display follow the non-camera slide. With two cameras, they follow the first in the pair.
- **Editing:** Pair settings shows which slide you're editing. Switching it only changes your own window. It never changes what the projector shows.
- **Hide one:** in Pair settings, the eye button beside a slide hides it (*Hide from screen; the other slide fills it*) and brings it back (*Show on screen*). You can still change the layout while one is hidden. It applies once the slide is shown again.
- **Swap the cameras:** in a pair of two cameras, this button in Pair settings flips which camera takes the picture's place.
- **Sound:** a camera's microphone is never played through the projector automatically, so the sound system can't feed back.

::callout{type="important" title="If your plan lapses"}
A pair is a Teams feature. If your church loses Teams, the pair stays in the schedule but can't be taken live until Teams returns.
::

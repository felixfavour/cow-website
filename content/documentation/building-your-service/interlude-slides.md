*Teams feature.* On the Free plan you can open the panel and try every animation. The upgrade prompt appears when you create the slide.

An interlude is an animated slide for the moments between segments: before service starts, during the offering, over announcements or while the band resets. A heading and a line of sub text stay on screen while the animation loops behind them.

## Create an interlude slide

1. Open **Add Interlude** in Quick Actions. Searching *break*, *welcome*, *fellowship* or *we'll be right back* finds it too.
2. Type a **Heading** (up to 60 characters) and, optionally, a **Sub text** (up to 70 characters). New interludes start with *Welcome Home* and *We'll begin shortly*.
3. Choose an **Animation**. Hover over a card to see it move.
4. Once you've picked one, **Create interlude slide** appears at the bottom. Click it and the slide is added to the schedule, ready to take live.

When you move from an interlude to the next slide, its text fades out before the next slide appears, so the change never feels abrupt.

## Animations

| Animation | Works well for |
|-----------|----------------|
| **CoW Original** | Communion, prayer, any service break |
| **Selah** | Countdowns to service, a pause between segments |
| **Still Waters** | Worship set intros, offering, reflection |
| **Lilies** | Easter, Mother's Day, women's fellowship |
| **Jubilee** | Celebration Sundays, youth nights, thanksgiving |
| **Gathering** | Welcome loops, pre-service, series titles |
| **Open Heavens** | Service start, conferences, youth |
| **Fellowship** | Announcements, events, fellowship breaks |

**CoW Original** is the default. Each animation comes with its own colours, so there is no separate background to pick.

## Text background

**Text background** controls the panel behind the heading and sub text:

- **Animation default** lets each animation decide.
- **Always on** keeps the text readable on busy animations or bright projectors.
- **Off** shows the text straight on the animation.

## Changing an interlude later

Select the slide in the schedule and open the **Interlude** tab in the Preview & Edit Pane. **Animation** switches the animation straight away. **Texts** lets you edit the heading, sub text and text background, then save. Interlude slides paint their own background, so the usual **Background** tab is not shown for them.

::callout{type="tip"}
An interlude is different from [Blank](/docs/going-live/blank-screen). Blank clears the Live Display for a moment and is always one key away. An interlude is a slide in your schedule with a message on it.
::

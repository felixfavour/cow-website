**My Library** is your permanent, schedule-independent store of reusable content.

## Two tabs

![My Library showing the Songs and Slides tabs and a saved song search](https://media.cloudofworship.com/open/marketing/docs/library-songs.webp)

| Tab | Contains | How things get here |
|-----|----------|---------------------|
| **Songs** | Every song you've saved | **Add Song**, or the save icon on a song slide |
| **Slides** | Saved text, media, hymn, interlude and presentation slides | A slide's **⋮ → Save to Library** |

## What you can do

- **Search**: *"Search all saved songs / slides"* filters the current tab.
- **Add to the schedule**: click any item.
- **Edit**: via **⋮ → Edit** (songs open in the Add Song editor).
- **Pin to top**: use **⋮ → Pin to top** on a song or slide to keep it at the top of its list, and **Unpin** to put it back. Pins are saved to your account, so they follow you to every computer.
- **Delete**: via **⋮ → Delete**, with confirmation. Deleting from your library is **not reversible**.

A presentation can be saved once all of its pages have uploaded, so the saved copy opens on every device. Adding a saved presentation back to a schedule reuses those uploaded pages and does not use more cloud storage.

Empty states tell you what to do. With no saved songs you'll see *"No songs saved yet. Click the save icon on the Slide card to start saving."*

::callout{type="tip"}
Long lists load progressively as you scroll (15 at a time), so a large song library stays fast. You can also navigate both tabs with your keyboard: the arrow keys move through the list, and **Enter** adds the focused song or slide to the schedule. Matching words are highlighted when you search.
::

## Import from EasyWorship

*Desktop app only.* Switching from EasyWorship? Search Quick Actions for **Import from EasyWorship** to bring your song library across.

1. In EasyWorship, make a backup with **File → Backup**.
2. In CoW, click **Choose EasyWorship backup (.zip)** and pick the file.
3. Wait for *Reading your song library…*, then choose which songs to keep (**Select all** and **Deselect all** help).
4. Confirm. CoW adds them to your library and tells you how many it added.

The backup is read on your own computer and never uploaded in full, so even backups of a gigabyte or more are fine. You need to be online to add the songs.

Large libraries stay quick: search is faster and slides load a page at a time, even with thousands of songs.

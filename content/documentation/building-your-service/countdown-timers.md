*Teams feature.*

![Countdown configuration](/images/docs/11-add-countdown-timer.webp)

1. Pick a **Countdown duration**, or set **hours : minutes : seconds** precisely.
2. Optionally add **text above your countdown**.
3. Click **Create countdown slide**.

::callout{type="tip"}
Need the current time instead? **Add Time Slide** displays the time of day and is available on the Free plan.
::

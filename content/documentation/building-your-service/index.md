Everything you need to put a church service together before Sunday: schedules for each service, Bible verses, song lyrics and hymns, announcement text slides, images and videos, countdown timers, camera feeds and PDF decks. You'll also find how to style a slide, add backgrounds, keep songs in your library and run a worship medley from a single setlist.

New to it? Start with [Schedules](/docs/building-your-service/schedules), then [Slide types](/docs/building-your-service/slide-types) to see what each Quick Action creates.

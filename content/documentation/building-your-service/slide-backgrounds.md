Click **Background** in the slide editor. Five sections:

| Section | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Add Image** | Upload or drag and drop a background image |
| **Add Video** | Upload or drag and drop a background video |
| **Add Colour** | Solid colour |
| **Add Gradient** | Gradient presets |
| **Background style** | **Blur** and **Brightness** sliders, plus how the media fills the slide |

**Fill types:** Fit · Stretch · Crop · Crop (Top) · Crop (Bottom)

## Upload limits depend on your plan

| | Free | Teams |
|---|------|-------|
| Background image | 3 MB | 10 MB |
| Background video | 250 MB | No limit |

::callout{type="tip" title="Keep lyrics readable"}
Use the **Brightness** slider to darken a busy photo so lyrics stay readable, rather than hunting for a darker image.
::

## Default backgrounds

You can set **default backgrounds per slide type** (Bible, hymn, text) in **Settings → Slide Background Settings**.

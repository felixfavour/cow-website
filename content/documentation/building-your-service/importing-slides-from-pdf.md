**Import Slides from PDF** converts an existing PDF (a printed program, a sermon deck exported to PDF) into slides, one per page.

Pages are converted to JPEG images of up to 1920 pixels across, so even large, photo-heavy pages upload quickly. If a page still fails to upload, CoW keeps it on your device and retries. See [Some pages didn't upload](/docs/troubleshooting/media-and-transcription-problems#some-pages-didnt-upload).

::callout{type="tip" title="Recent Presentations"}
CoW remembers decks you've already imported **on this device** and offers them for quick reuse, so you don't have to re-upload the same PDF for a repeating series. This list is local to the device you imported on, not synced to your church workspace.
::

When something goes wrong with Cloud of Worship, we write it up here: what happened, who was affected, how we fixed it, and what we changed so it doesn't happen again. We publish these because churches rely on us every week and deserve to know what happened.

For problems on your own setup, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

Running a holiday Bible club or vacation Bible school while managing campus lectures and fellowship responsibilities tests your planning. Student fellowships rarely own permanent buildings, so you are likely working with borrowed lecture rooms, shared sound gear, and volunteers who must balance revision timetables with ministry. This checklist keeps your team organized from planning week through teardown, ensuring every child is safe, every lesson runs smoothly, and your volunteer team avoids panic.

## Two weeks before

- [ ] Book the venue with university security or room coordinators, confirming access times and power availability.
- [ ] Appoint key team roles: club director, head of games, worship coordinator, registration lead, and first-aid volunteer.
- [ ] Draft a clear daily schedule, breaking four-hour mornings into 20-minute focused blocks.
- [ ] Build your song list and memory verse slides; in Cloud of Worship, create your schedule in a shared workspace so teaching and music leads can edit slides together.
- [ ] Print child registration sheets asking for emergency contacts, allergies, and parent pick-up authorizations.
- [ ] Confirm clean drinking water supply, disposable cups, and basic first-aid supplies (plasters, antiseptic liquid, paracetamol, cotton wool).
- [ ] Assign two student leaders per age bracket to keep a reliable adult-to-child supervision ratio.
- [ ] Test all games on your team beforehand to confirm rules, timing, and equipment needs.

## Three days before

- [ ] Do a physical gear check: inspect HDMI cords, power extension strips, projector brightness, microphones, and audio leads.
- [ ] Download all media assets to your computer; load your presentation schedule in Cloud of Worship so it runs offline if campus Wi-Fi drops.
- [ ] Gather craft supplies into labeled plastic containers for each group table.
- [ ] Send text or WhatsApp reminders to registered parents with drop-off times, venue landmarks, and what children should bring.
- [ ] Walk the hall to identify safety hazards: loose electrical cables, broken chair legs, or open access doors.
- [ ] Hold a 30-minute briefing with all student volunteers to run through emergency exits and toilet chaperoning rules.

## Morning setup (7:30 AM)

- [ ] Arrive 90 minutes before children enter; unlock windows, turn on fans, and sweep floor surfaces.
- [ ] Plug in the projector, extend the display, and test your lyrics output against your live wall or screen.
- [ ] Set up the registration desk right at the entrance with sign-in sheets, wristbands, and name badges.
- [ ] Test microphone batteries and set volume levels so speakers do not have to shout over energetic room noise.
- [ ] Lay out group seating mats or chair clusters with room for children to move during action songs.
- [ ] Place the first-aid kit, spare dry clothes, and bottled water behind the central check-in desk.
- [ ] Gather the volunteer team at 8:15 AM for a five-minute prayer and role recap.

## During the sessions

- [ ] Start a five-minute on-screen countdown timer to draw children to their seats without shouting.
- [ ] Keep song sessions active; project lyrics clearly so newer children can follow every phrase.
- [ ] Use presentation banner alerts to display parent numbers on screen if a child needs attention, avoiding spoken interruptions.
- [ ] Enforce the two-leader rule: no adult is ever alone in a side room or bathroom with a child.
- [ ] Rotate between loud games and quiet craft activities to preserve volunteer energy and child attention.
- [ ] Serve pre-portioned snacks seated at tables to avoid spills and food-sharing conflicts.
- [ ] Hand out memory verse sheets based on Proverbs 22:6 right before group dismissal.
- [ ] Match parent claim tickets or signatures at the door before releasing any child at pick-up.

## After the club ends

- [ ] Return all borrowed lecture hall furniture to its original layout and sweep floors clean.
- [ ] Inventory leftover craft materials, snacks, and medical items; store them in sealed plastic bins.
- [ ] Back up child registration records and parent contact lists for future fellowship follow-up.
- [ ] Coil and pack all microphone leads, HDMI cables, and extension blocks neatly.
- [ ] Hold a 15-minute debrief with your student team to note what worked and what needs adjustment.
- [ ] Send a thank-you message to parents with a photo summary and an invite to regular fellowship services.

Adapt this checklist by shifting times to fit afternoon sessions or scaling group sizes based on the number of student volunteers available.

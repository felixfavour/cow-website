A countdown before service starts belongs on the main screen. A five-minute cue for the worship team between segments usually does not. Cloud of Worship lets the operator send a countdown to the Stage Display while the congregation continues seeing the current live slide.

The [Stage Display](/docs/going-live/stage-display) is the confidence monitor facing your musicians and speakers. It shows what is live, what is next, a timer and the time of day. Opening the display is free. Sending a private countdown to it is a Teams feature.

## Pick the right destination

| Your goal | Choose | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Let the congregation know when service begins | **Live Display** | A countdown slide is added to the schedule and can be taken live. |
| Give the band or speaker a time cue without changing projection | **Stage Display** | A countdown starts on the confidence monitor only. No slide is added. |
| Track how long a service or segment has run | **Stage Clock** | The stage stopwatch counts up. |

The stage panel shows a private stage countdown first. If there is none, it mirrors a countdown slide that is live. Otherwise it shows the service stopwatch. This order keeps the stage cue visible even when the congregation's screen is showing something else.

## Start a private countdown

Use the presentation computer that is driving the Stage Display:

1. Open **Quick Actions** and search for **Stage Display Countdown**.
2. In the **Stage Display** tab, choose a duration or set the minutes and seconds yourself.
3. Optionally add a short message beneath the timer, such as *Band back in five minutes*.
4. Click **Start stage countdown**.

For a quick cue, type *5 min countdown* in Quick Actions. You will see separate instant actions for the live output and Stage Display. Choose the stage action when the congregation should not see the timer.

The Stage Display's buttons can pause, resume or reset its private countdown. You can also search for **Start Stage Clock**, **Pause Stage Clock** or **Restart Stage Clock** in the Control Center. Those actions operate whichever stage clock is currently showing.

## Return to the service stopwatch

Search for **Clear Stage Countdown** when the private cue is finished. The service stopwatch keeps running underneath the countdown, so clearing the cue restores its current reading. If the stopwatch was paused before the cue, it returns at the same paused reading.

A private countdown stays at zero until you clear or restart it. Clearing it also lets the Stage Display mirror a live countdown slide again, if one is on the congregation's screen.

The stage clock is controlled from the presentation computer. A phone can add a countdown slide to the shared schedule, but its Quick Actions do not control the Stage Display clock on another device.

For the full setup and both countdown paths, see the [Countdown timers guide](/docs/building-your-service/countdown-timers) and [Multi-screen setup](/docs/going-live/multi-screen-setup).
